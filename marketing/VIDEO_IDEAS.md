# The First 12 Videos

Every video uses real gameplay. **Numbers in [brackets] come from your recording**: never type a number, grade or
result the footage doesn't show. If a concept needs a moment that hasn't happened yet (for example a good call that
failed), keep recording until it does, or skip that concept.

**Conditional** in the table means the video depends on a moment that may not happen in a given session. **Mode**
says where the footage can come from: Career is the fictional league (safe while the real-names decision is open);
the other modes show real rosters.

Shared pieces (set up once in the CapCut template, see [CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md](CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md)):
- **End card** (last 2–2.5 s): [assets/end-card.png](assets/end-card.png): "Link in bio", the site address, and
  "Free in your browser · No sign-up". On Reddit, where there is no bio link, put the link in the post instead.
- **Bio link:** tagged per platform with `utm_content` set to the video ID below.
- **Text style:** hook in all caps, white on a dark box, the key number in yellow #F5C518.
- **Branding rule:** no team names, player names or league marks in titles, hooks, captions or hashtags.

## v01: 4th & 1, down 3. What's your call?

- **Duration:** 18–22 s
- **Hook (0–1.5 s):** frozen scoreboard and field. Text: **"4TH & 1. DOWN 3. [1:39] LEFT. YOUR CALL?"**
- **Footage:** a Daily or Practice game that starts on fourth down (the Daily's "Fourth-down call" type, or build it
  in Practice). Record the brief, the call panel with the kick and go options visible, the play, and the result card.
- **Timeline:**
  - 0.0–1.5 Freeze on the scoreboard and field (WIN % visible). Hook text.
  - 1.5–4.0 Slow zoom on the call panel options. Caption: "Kick it? Punt? Go for it?"
  - 4.0–5.5 Text: "Comment your call 👇" (hold 1.5 s; this is the pause that earns comments).
  - 5.5–6.5 Your choice highlighted. Caption: "I went with [call]."
  - 6.5–12.0 The play, real speed. Whistle at the snap.
  - 12.0–17.0 Zoom on the result card. Caption: "The game graded it: [Great call / Close / Costly / Big miss]."
    If it shows a better call: "It wanted [best call], +[N] pts win chance."
  - 17.0–19.5 End card.
- **Captions (exact):** "4TH & 1. DOWN 3. [1:39] LEFT. YOUR CALL?" / "Kick it? Punt? Go for it?" / "Comment your call 👇" /
  "I went with [call]." / "The game graded it: [grade]." / "It wanted [best call]: +[N] pts win chance."
- **Voice-over (optional):** "Fourth and one, down three, a minute thirty-nine left. What do you call? … I went for it.
  Here's what the game thought of that."
- **Sound:** low crowd bed; whistle on the snap; a single "ding" on the result card.
- **Ending / CTA:** end card. Pinned comment: "Same spot is in today's game. Free in the browser, link in bio."
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "4th and 1, down 3. Be honest: what are you calling? #footballtok #footballstrategy #playcalling"
  - Reels: "4th & 1, down 3, under two minutes. Your call? #football #footballstrategy #playcalling"
  - Shorts: "4th & 1, down 3: what's your call? #football #footballstrategy #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** a decision question in the first frame drives comments and rewatches. Measure comment rate and
  3-second hold against your median.

## v02: The right call that didn't work

- **Duration:** 15–20 s
- **Hook:** the failed play's last frame. Text: **"THIS FAILED. THE GAME STILL SAYS IT WAS RIGHT."**
- **Footage:** any play where the result card shows **Great call** but the play lost yards, fell incomplete or
  turned the ball over. Only use it if it actually happens.
- **Timeline:**
  - 0.0–2.0 Freeze on the bad outcome plus the hook text.
  - 2.0–4.0 Rewind cut to the situation: scoreboard, down and distance. Caption: "[3rd & 6], [0:52] left."
  - 4.0–9.0 The play. Caption: "I called [play]."
  - 9.0–14.0 Result card zoom: Great call. Caption: "Every call is graded on the decision, not the result."
  - 14.0–16.5 End card.
- **Captions:** "THIS FAILED. THE GAME STILL SAYS IT WAS RIGHT." / "[3rd & 6], [0:52] left." / "I called [play]." /
  "Great call. It graded the decision, not the result."
- **Voice-over:** "This didn't work. The game still calls it the right call, because it grades the decision against
  every other option, not the outcome."
- **Sound:** crowd groan on the failure (licensed SFX), a beat of silence, then a "ding" on Great call.
- **Ending / CTA:** end card. Pinned comment: "Agree or disagree with the grade?"
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "Good call, bad result. Does it still count? #footballtok #playcalling #footballstrategy"
  - Reels: "Process over results: the play failed, the call was right. #footballstrategy #playcalling"
  - Shorts: "The play failed. The call was right. #footballstrategy #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** the "graded on the decision" idea is the game's most distinctive feature. Measure shares and
  replies that argue with the grade.

## v03: The wrong call that worked anyway

- **Duration:** 15–20 s
- **Hook:** a big gain or touchdown. Text: **"THIS WORKED. THE GAME CALLED IT A BIG MISS."**
- **Footage:** a play graded **Costly** or **Big miss** that gained a lot. Only if it happens.
- **Timeline:** the mirror of v02. Hook on the success (0–2 s), the situation (2–4 s), the play (4–9 s), then the
  result card with "Better call: [X] +[N] pts" (9–14 s), then the end card.
- **Captions:** "THIS WORKED. THE GAME CALLED IT A BIG MISS." / "[situation]" / "I called [play]. It hit." /
  "[Big miss]: [best call] was worth +[N] pts of win chance."
- **Voice-over:** "Touchdown. And the game still says I blew it. Here's why."
- **Sound:** crowd pop on the gain, then a record-scratch-style stop (licensed) on the grade.
- **Ending / CTA:** pinned comment: "Was the game wrong here?"
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "Scored. Still graded a big miss. Fair? #footballtok #playcalling"
  - Reels: "Result: great. Call: bad. #footballstrategy #playcalling"
  - Shorts: "It worked, but it was the wrong call. #football #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** the controversy version of v02. If it out-comments v02, viewers like arguing with the grade more than
  being reassured by it.

## v04: Spike it or burn a timeout?

- **Duration:** 20–25 s
- **Hook:** frozen clock running. Text: **"[0:41] AND RUNNING. 1 TIMEOUT. SPIKE OR TIMEOUT?"**
- **Footage:** a two-minute drill where you complete an in-bounds pass with the clock running and must choose: Spike,
  Timeout or a tempo (No-huddle). Show the clock options on screen.
- **Timeline:**
  - 0.0–2.0 Hook on the running clock.
  - 2.0–5.0 Zoom on the clock choices. Caption: "Spike costs a down. A timeout costs a timeout."
  - 5.0–6.5 "What would you do? 👇"
  - 6.5–8.0 Your choice. Caption: "I [spiked it]."
  - 8.0–15.0 The next play or two at 1.5×, the clock visible.
  - 15.0–21.0 Result card for the clock call. Caption: "[grade]. [Best call] was +[N] pts."
  - 21.0–23.5 End card.
- **Captions:** as above, plus a final "Clock calls get graded too."
- **Voice-over:** "Forty-one seconds, running, one timeout. Spike it and lose a down, or burn your last timeout?"
- **Sound:** a clock tick under the freeze (only during the freeze), a whistle on the spike.
- **Ending / CTA:** "Clock management is half the game. Link in bio."
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "Spike or timeout? There's a right answer (according to the game). #footballtok #clockmanagement"
  - Reels: "Running clock, one timeout. Spike or timeout? #footballstrategy #clockmanagement"
  - Shorts: "Spike or timeout with 0:41 left? #football #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** clock-management fans are a narrower but more engaged audience. Measure game starts per 1,000 views
  against v01.

## v05: Sideline or middle?

- **Duration:** 25–30 s
- **Hook:** two routes drawn on the field. Text: **"THE SIDELINE STOPS THE CLOCK. THE MIDDLE GAINS MORE."**
- **Footage:** a drive late in the game. Record one pass with the **Sideline** signal or an out route, and a separate
  play over the middle (dig or crosser), both with the clock visible. Two clips from the same or different sessions.
- **Timeline:**
  - 0.0–2.5 Hook over the field.
  - 2.5–10.0 Clip A: an out route or the Sideline signal. Caption: "Out of bounds: clock stops. [+N] yards."
  - 10.0–17.0 Clip B: over the middle. Caption: "Middle: [+N] yards, clock keeps running ([0:xx] gone)."
  - 17.0–24.0 One result card from either clip. Caption: "The game graded both. [Grade A] vs [Grade B]."
  - 24.0–26.5 End card.
- **Captions:** as listed. Never claim one is always better.
- **Voice-over:** "Sideline stops the clock. The middle gets more yards and keeps it running. Which one you want depends on
  the clock, and the game grades it every time."
- **Sound:** whistle when the runner goes out of bounds; a tick while the clock runs on clip B.
- **Ending / CTA:** "Try both in today's game. Link in bio."
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "Sideline or middle with a minute left? #footballtok #twominutedrill"
  - Reels: "The two-minute drill in one decision. #footballstrategy #twominutedrill"
  - Shorts: "Sideline vs middle in the two-minute drill. #football #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** an explainer format. Measure average watch time; explainers should hold viewers longer even with fewer
  comments.

## v06: Can you score from here before time runs out?

- **Duration:** 30–35 s (longer because it's a whole drive)
- **Hook:** the starting spot. Text: **"[1:20] LEFT. [OWN 25]. NEED A TOUCHDOWN. GO."**
- **Footage:** one complete drive, from the brief to the final whistle. Daily "Dig out of the hole" or a Practice spot.
- **Timeline:**
  - 0.0–2.0 Hook, frozen on the brief or scoreboard.
  - 2.0–28.0 The drive at 2–3× between snaps, real speed for each play's key moment. A small corner counter:
    "[0:58] · [3rd & 4]". Cut every 2–3 s.
  - 28.0–31.0 The final result (score or stop), real speed.
  - 31.0–33.5 End card.
- **Captions:** the hook, then the clock and the down at each cut, then the honest outcome: "Touchdown with [0:04] left" or
  "Stopped at the [12]."
- **Voice-over:** none. Let the clock carry it.
- **Sound:** a ticking bed that speeds up slightly near the end; a whistle on each snap is too much, so use it only on
  the last snap.
- **Ending / CTA:** "Same game for everyone today. Can you do it? Link in bio."
- **Platform captions:**
  - TikTok: "[1:20] to go 75 yards. Did I make it? #footballtok #twominutedrill #indiegame"
  - Reels: "Full two-minute drill in 30 seconds. #twominutedrill #footballstrategy"
  - Shorts: "Two-minute drill: score or lose. #football #shorts"
- **Hypothesis:** a complete arc with a payoff drives completion rate. Measure completion and average watch time.

  - 25.0–27.5 End card: "Daily resets at midnight UTC."

- **Ending / CTA:** "Post your score in the comments." Post the video the same day, before midnight UTC.

- **Platform captions:**

  - Reels: "One loss and it's over. [N] in a row. #footballstrategy #challenge"
