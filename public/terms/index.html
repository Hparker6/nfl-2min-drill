Terms & refunds

Last updated October 21, 2026. These are short on purpose. If something here is unclear, ask through Help & refunds in the game.

The game

The 4th Call is a free football play-calling game in your browser, made by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the NFL, any of its teams, or the NFL Players Association. Team and player names identify real teams and players; no logos or marks are used. Outcomes, ratings and win chances are the game's own estimates from a simplified simulation.

You may play it for personal, non-commercial use. Don't try to break it, overload it, scrape it, or get around the sign-in and purchase checks. The game is provided as is: we work to keep it up and bug-free, but can't promise it will always be available or that saved progress in your browser will never be lost.

Accounts

An account is optional. It uses your email address and no password: a sign-in link and code are emailed to you. An account exists so a purchase can follow you to another device; your saved games stay in each browser. You can delete your account at any time from the Account panel. What we store and why is in About & privacy in the game.

The Coach's Edition

The Coach's Edition is a one-time purchase of $4.99 (USD) for one account. There is no subscription and no renewal. It unlocks the features listed on the purchase screen (such as no ads, Career Legacy, more career slots and the Gridiron Challenge ladder). We may add to the edition; we won't take away what you bought.

Payment is taken by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your card details. The price shown at checkout is the price; any tax Stripe adds is shown before you pay.

Refunds

The edition is a digital product delivered instantly, so a refund is not owed once it is unlocked. Even so:

  • One purchase per account is refunded on request within 14 days of the purchase, no reason needed. Ask through Help & refunds in the game (footer, or the Account panel) with the email you bought with. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe, usually within 5-10 business days.
  • When a purchase is refunded, the edition is removed from the account.
  • A purchase made after a refund is final.
  • If you ask your bank to reverse a charge instead (a chargeback), the edition is removed and purchases are closed for that account. A chargeback costs us about four times the sale; please ask us first, we're quick.
  • Where consumer law in your country gives you more than this, that law applies.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. A change never shrinks what a purchase you already made includes.

Contact

Use Help & refunds in the game. Every request gets a reference number and an answer by email, usually within a day or two.
