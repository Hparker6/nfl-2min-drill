Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026. The 4th Call is a free browser game made by an independent developer in Texas, USA. This page says what it stores, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. Questions: Help & refunds in the game.

Without an account

You can play everything that's free without an account. Your games, streaks, stats, careers, badges and settings are kept in your browser (its local storage), not on our servers. Clearing your browser's data, or Settings → Erase my data, removes them.

The daily leaderboard

If you post a Daily score, our server stores the nickname you chose, your score, your call grades, whether you won, whether you used a Coach's Playbook play, and a random code your browser made up (so a second post replaces the first). The top names and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard. To limit posts, your IP address is counted for that day as a one-way hash; the address itself isn't kept.

Accounts (optional)

An account exists so a purchase can follow you to another device. It uses your email address and no password. We store:

  • your email address and when the account was made;
  • your signed-in sessions, kept as one-way hashes of random keys. A cookie (__Host-4c_sid, not readable by scripts) keeps you signed in; it ends after 30 days without use, or 180 days at most;
  • recent sign-in requests (the emailed link and code are stored only as one-way hashes, and deleted about a day after they expire);
  • counters that limit repeated requests, keyed by one-way hashes of your email and IP address.

The sign-in and help forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that you're a person, which Cloudflare runs.

Purchases

The Coach's Edition is paid on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your card details. We keep a record of each purchase: the product, the amount and currency, the date, Stripe's reference numbers, the Stripe customer it belongs to, whether it was refunded or disputed, and a one-way hash of the buyer's email, so the refund history stays with the address even if an account is deleted and made again. Stripe keeps its own records under Stripe's privacy policy.

Help & refunds requests

When you send a request we store what you wrote, the email you gave, the kind of request, where in the game you were, and your account if you were signed in, and email it to the developer so it can be answered.

Analytics and ads

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, counts visits with its web analytics.
  • Google Analytics may receive gameplay events (for example, that a game started or a result was shared), never names, emails or contact details. Google may set cookies for it.
  • Google AdSense shows ads on some screens and may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure them. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, ads and analytics wait for your consent. Owners of the Coach's Edition see no ads, and their browser doesn't load the ad script. How Google uses this: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Who else handles it

We don't sell your information. These services process it for us, only to run the game: Cloudflare (hosting, the database, Turnstile, analytics), Stripe (payments), Resend (sign-in and help emails) and Google (fonts, analytics, ads). Each also has its own privacy policy. Like any website, our hosting sees IP addresses to deliver the pages; our own logs record what happened (a purchase, a refund) without emails, codes or keys.

How long, and deleting it

  • Delete your account any time: Account → Delete my account. That removes your email address, sessions, sign-in requests and what you own. Purchase records stay, without your email (only its one-way hash), for refunds, disputes, and tax and accounting records.
  • Leaderboard entries stay as part of that day's board. To have one removed, ask through Help & refunds.
  • Help requests are kept while they're open and as long as needed afterwards. You can ask for yours to be deleted.
  • To ask for a copy of what we hold about you, a correction or a deletion, use Help & refunds with the email involved. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under your local law; we'll honor them.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has made an account or sent us their email, ask through Help & refunds and we'll delete it.
