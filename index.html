The 4th Call: a free 2-minute drill play-calling game

A free football strategy game: run the two-minute drill at the end of a close American football game. Call every play from a 27-play book of runs, passes, RPOs and trick plays, manage the clock and your timeouts, and call the defense from 19 coverages and blitzes when they have the ball. Teams use real NFL rosters and season stats.

Every call is graded against every option you had, by playing the rest of the game out thousands of times: a great call is within 0.75 points of win chance of the best one, close within 2.5, costly within 6, and a big miss beyond that. A good call that fails still counts as good.

Four ways to play: the Daily is one game, the same for everyone, new at midnight UTC, with a leaderboard. Survivor deals random late-game spots back to back until you lose one. Practice lets you build any situation and shows every option's win chance in Coach mode. Career takes a quarterback you create through one college game, the draft and a full season in a fictional league. Free in your browser, no account, no download.
