NFL 2-Minute Drill: call the plays in The 4th Call

Run the NFL 2 min drill at the end of a close game. Call every play, manage the clock and your timeouts, and call the defense when they have the ball. Teams use real NFL rosters and season stats.

Every call is graded against every option you had, by playing the rest of the game out thousands of times. Free in your browser, no sign-up, and a new daily game for everyone, plus Survivor and Practice modes.
